## Executive Summary

The financial planning software market is dominated by three legacy platforms—eMoney Advisor, MoneyGuidePro (Envestnet), and RightCapital—that collectively control approximately 80% of the advisor market. However, these platforms face significant challenges: aging technology architectures, declining user satisfaction, and vulnerability to AI-native disruption.

Fiduciary OS™ enters this market with a differentiated value proposition: an AI-powered, dual-sided platform that integrates financial planning with insurance product optimization, while simultaneously serving both professional advisors and consumer households. This analysis maps the competitive landscape, identifies Fiduciary OS™'s sustainable advantages, and outlines strategies for competitive positioning.

**Key Findings:**
- Legacy platforms show vulnerability: MoneyGuidePro lost 10% market share (33% → 23%) in 2 years
- User satisfaction scores favor newer entrants: RightCapital (8.52/10) vs. MoneyGuidePro (7.67/10)
- No dominant AI-native competitor has emerged—window remains open
- Fiduciary OS™'s dual-sided model creates unique network effects

|---------|------------------------------------------|
| Likelihood | Medium |
| Impact | Medium-High |

**Response Strategy**:
1. Emphasize total cost of ownership (replace 6-8 tools)
2. Highlight AI capabilities that justify premium
3. Offer implementation services to reduce switching friction
4. Accelerate partnership revenue to offset price pressure

### 7.2 Scenario: RightCapital Accelerates Growth

| Trigger | RightCapital raises significant funding, aggressive expansion |
|---------|-------------------------------------------------------------|
| Likelihood | High |
| Impact | High |

**Response Strategy**:
1. Differentiate through insurance integration (RightCapital weakness)
2. Accelerate B2C launch to establish network effects
3. Target advisors with insurance-heavy practices
4. Emphasize Dynamic Source Router™ differentiation
