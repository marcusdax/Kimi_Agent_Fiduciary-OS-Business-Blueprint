# User Guide — Fiduciary OS Educational Program
**Version:** 1.0 · November 14, 2026  
**Audience:** Learners, educators, institutional administrators

## Welcome

Fiduciary OS is an educational platform that helps you understand financial planning concepts. It is **not** personalized advice. Every tool and explanation includes a clear disclaimer.

## Getting Started

1. Create a free account (or use an institutional seat).  
2. Complete the short orientation that explains the educational boundary.  
3. Explore the modules at your own pace.

## Core Modules (Educational)

- **Financial Security Score** — An educational gauge of common planning factors (not a credit score or advice).  
- **Protection Gap Explainer** — Concepts of income replacement, debt coverage, and education funding.  
- **Investment Literacy** — Risk concepts, fee drag, and diversification ideas (illustrative only).  
- **Retirement Income Concepts** — Social Security timing ideas, withdrawal frameworks, and “questions to ask an advisor.”  
- **AI Tutor** — Explains terms and shows examples. It will never tell you what to buy or do.

## For Educators & Administrators

- Assign modules or learning paths.  
- View aggregate completion and engagement (no individual financial data).  
- Download printable educational summaries (all carry the disclaimer).  
- Request additional seats or convert a pilot under the educational license.

## Important Reminders

- All numbers are illustrative or hypothetical.  
- Always consult a licensed professional for your personal situation.  
- The platform does not sell or recommend specific products.  
- Institutional use is limited to educational purposes.
