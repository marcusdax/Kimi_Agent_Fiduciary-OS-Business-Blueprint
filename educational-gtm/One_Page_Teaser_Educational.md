# One-Page Teaser — Fiduciary OS Educational Program
**November 14, 2026 | Pre-revenue · Education-only positioning**

Fiduciary OS is building the operating system for financial literacy. We launch exclusively as an educational program—persistent disclaimers, no advice, no certifications, no licensing required—so we can move immediately into public-sector channels that need trustworthy planning education without regulatory risk.

American households face $35T+ in retirement assets, 10,000 daily new retirees, and fragmented tools. Most existing software either sells product or claims to give advice. We teach concepts, frameworks, and the right questions, using clear illustrative outputs and AI explainers that stay strictly educational.

**How we grow (density-first):**
1. Win dense public-sector clusters (workforce boards, community colleges, veteran services, libraries) with free or low-cost educational pilots.
2. Open multi-tier consumer subscriptions ($0 / $9–15 mo) once institutions prove demand.
3. License the same educational modules to advisors and large enterprises—and position for strategic acquisition.

The current prototype already enforces education-only AI prompts, output guardrails, and illustrative labeling. All revenue in the near term is pure subscription; no product-placement or advice-linked fees.

We are pre-launch. The educational path is the lowest-friction way to generate density, data, and brand before commercial licensing.
