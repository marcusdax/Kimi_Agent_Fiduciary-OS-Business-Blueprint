# Fiduciary OS™ — Educational Program GTM Memo
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Classification:** Internal / Pre-Seed Strategy  
**Status:** Proposed density-first path — educational program only

---

## 1. Opportunity

The original dual-sided Advisor Command Center + consumer portal vision carries material securities and insurance licensing risk. By launching exclusively as an **educational financial-literacy program** with persistent, prominent disclaimers, Fiduciary OS can:

- Remove the need for any certification, licensing, or “fiduciary advice” claims at launch.
- Become immediately promotable to public-sector buyers (libraries, workforce boards, community colleges, veteran services, state literacy grants).
- Build density and usage data that later unlocks commercial licensing to advisors and large enterprises, creating a path to acquisition optionality.

All current prototype strengths (education-only AI prompts, output guardrails, illustrative labeling, provenance controls) already support this positioning.

## 2. Positioning & Product Boundary

**What it is:**  
A multi-tiered educational platform that teaches financial planning concepts, frameworks, and “questions to ask.” Tools produce clearly labeled illustrative or hypothetical outputs only.

**What it is not:**  
Personalized advice, recommendations, product placement, certified planning, or any service that requires a securities, insurance, or investment-adviser license.

**Non-negotiable language rule:** Every user-facing surface (UI, AI responses, emails, marketing, institutional contracts) carries the approved disclaimer stack (see `Disclaimer_Language.md`).

## 3. Density-First Go-to-Market

### Phase 0–1: Public-Sector Educational Clusters (Months 1–12)
Select 1–2 high-hunger, low-friction verticals and win them densely before any broad consumer spend.

Priority clusters [ASSUMPTION — founder judgment]:
- State/local workforce & financial-literacy programs
- Community-college adult education systems
- Veteran / military transition offices
- Public library systems

**Motion:**
- Zero- or low-cost institutional pilots under pure educational terms.
- Measure nodes (active learners + institutions) and edges (educator sharing, cross-agency referrals, repeat sessions).
- Success gate: 3–5 reference institutions with documented engagement before scaling paid acquisition.

### Phase 2: Consumer Educational Subscription Tiers (Months 6–18)
Once public density exists, open freemium + paid educational tiers for households.

| Tier | Price [ASSUMPTION] | Core offering |
|------|---------------------|---------------|
| Free | $0 | Basic calculators + concept explainers + Financial Security Score (educational) |
| Premium | $9–15 / month | Full module library, progress tracking, family sharing, printable educational reports, AI tutoring on concepts |

All tiers remain education-only.

### Phase 3: Commercial Licensing (Months 12–30+)
After measured educational traction and formal counsel review:
- White-label or site-licensed educational modules for RIAs, agencies, and broker-dealers (they operate under their own compliance).
- Enterprise / corporate licenses for banks, credit unions, employers, and insurers (financial-wellness programs).
- Explicit acquisition-readiness: density + consented educational dataset + brand in public and consumer channels.

2. Public-sector buyers adopt and renew on literacy value alone. [ASSUMPTION]
