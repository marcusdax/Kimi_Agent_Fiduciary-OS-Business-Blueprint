## Headline
**Finally, financial literacy tools that stay educational.**

## Subhead
Fiduciary OS gives households and public programs clear concepts, frameworks, and illustrative examples—without advice, certifications, or licensing complexity.

## The Problem
People need to understand Social Security timing, protection gaps, fees, and retirement income concepts. Most tools either sell products or claim to give advice. Public programs need something trustworthy they can actually use.

## The Solution
An education-only platform with:
- Concept explainers and “questions to ask”
- Illustrative calculators (clearly labeled)
- AI tutor that stays educational
- Persistent disclaimers on every screen
- Institutional admin tools for libraries, colleges, workforce, and veteran programs

## How It Works for Institutions
1. Start with a free 90-day educational pilot.  
2. Learners explore modules at their own pace.  
3. You see aggregate engagement.  
4. Expand seats or convert to a low-cost site license when it proves valuable.

## Pricing (Educational Tiers)

## Why Now
Public financial-literacy mandates and grant programs are growing. An education-only product removes the usual regulatory barriers so programs can deploy immediately.
