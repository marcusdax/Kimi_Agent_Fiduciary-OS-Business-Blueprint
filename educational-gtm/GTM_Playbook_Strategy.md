# GTM Playbook Strategy — Fiduciary OS Educational Program
**Version:** 1.0 · November 14, 2026  
**Audience:** Founders, early operators, future sales/success hires

## 1. Strategic Objective
Establish measurable density in 1–2 public-sector educational clusters within 12 months, then open consumer subscription tiers, then unlock commercial licensing. No paid acquisition until density is proven.

## 2. Ideal Customer Profiles (Density Phase)

| Cluster | Buyer | Pain | Why education-only works |
|---------|-------|------|--------------------------|
| Workforce / literacy programs | Program director | Need trusted, free or low-cost tools for participants | No licensing friction |
| Community colleges | Adult-ed coordinator | Students need practical planning literacy | Fits existing curriculum |
| Veteran services | Transition office | Service members need clear frameworks before decisions | High trust requirement |
| Public libraries | Adult services | Patrons request financial education | Easy distribution |

## 3. Pilot Playbook (First 90 Days)

1. **Select** two clusters and three named targets each.  
2. **Outreach** with one-page teaser + disclaimer package + 15-minute demo of educational tools only.  
3. **Offer** 90-day free pilot (up to 200 seats) under educational license.  
4. **Success metrics:** weekly active learners, session completion, educator NPS, request to expand or pay.  
5. **Exit criteria:** ≥60 % of pilots request continuation or paid conversion before any consumer marketing spend.

## 4. Channel Sequence

- **Now:** Direct founder outreach + warm intros to program directors.  
- **Months 3–9:** Educator referral network + case-study sharing inside the cluster.  
- **Months 6+:** Consumer freemium opened to households exposed by institutions.  
- **Months 12+:** Commercial licensing conversations (advisors, enterprises) only after counsel sign-off.  
- **Never first:** Broad paid digital, advisor cold outreach, or product-placement partnerships.

## 5. Content & Enablement

- Educational modules only (concept explainers, “questions to ask,” illustrative calculators).  
- Institutional one-pager and admin guide.  
- Weekly office hours for pilot educators.  
- All materials carry the short-form disclaimer.

## 6. Measurement Dashboard

- Nodes: institutions live, active learners / week  
- Edges: educator-to-educator shares, cross-agency referrals  
- Quality: session completion rate, qualitative feedback  
- Economics: cost per institution, free-to-paid conversion (once opened)

## 7. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Pilot fatigue | Strict 90-day scope + clear success metrics |
| Language drift into advice | Weekly disclaimer audit + AI guardrail tests |
| Slow public procurement | Start with grant-funded or discretionary budgets |
| Density too thin | Do not expand to second vertical until first is dense |

## 8. Resource Plan (Seed Use)

- 40 % product (educational UX, disclaimers, admin tools)  
- 25 % public-sector success (one dedicated operator)  
- 20 % legal / counsel review  
- 15 % content & measurement
