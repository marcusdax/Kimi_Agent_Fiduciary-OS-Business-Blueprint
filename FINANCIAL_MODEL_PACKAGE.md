# Fiduciary OS Financial Model Package

**Version:** 2.0 · **Updated:** October 2026 · **Supersedes:** v1.0 (December 2024)

*Confidential. Prepared for the Fiduciary OS management team and prospective investors. All figures are projections for a company that has not launched; none are results.*

---

## What changed in v2

| Area | v1 (December 2024) | v2 (October 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Performance-partnership revenue | Revenue share of 25–45% on placed insurance premium, ~$7.0M in Year 5 | **Removed.** Commission splits and per-placement revenue share generally require an insurance license; this revenue is not modelled until counsel has reviewed the structure and a licensed channel partner is named |
| LTV | ARPU × 600 months, no gross margin (Solo LTV $59,400) | ARPU × gross margin ÷ monthly churn (Solo LTV $4,950) |
| LTV/CAC | 90–120× | **8.5–15.0×** |
| Operating expenses | Scaled with revenue | Same dollar plan as v1; lower revenue widens losses |
| Taxes | 25% on positive income, no carryforward | 25% after net-operating-loss carryforward |
| Equity financing | $2M / $5M / $10M (did not match the funding plan) | $5M Seed (Year 1) + $15M Series A (Year 2), matching [Funding_Strategy.md](Funding_Strategy.md) |
| Year 5 ARR target in the investor summary | $51.7M (did not match the model) | $19.95M — one number everywhere |
| Reproducibility | Static CSVs | Generated by [`model/build_model.py`](model/build_model.py) |

Regenerate everything with:

```bash
python3 model/build_model.py
```

## Base case

| Metric | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 | Year 4 | Year 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Revenue** | $1.44M | $4.05M | $8.00M | $13.59M | $21.05M |
| B2B (advisor SaaS) | $0.79M | $1.96M | $3.64M | $5.94M | $8.90M |
| B2C (premium subscriptions) | $0.65M | $2.08M | $4.36M | $7.64M | $12.15M |
| **ARR (end of year)** | $1.37M | $3.85M | $7.59M | $12.89M | $19.95M |
| Revenue growth | — | 181% | 98% | 70% | 55% |
| Gross margin | 70% | 74% | 77% | 79% | 80% |
| **EBITDA** | −$1.11M | −$1.66M | −$1.36M | −$0.16M | $1.99M |
| EBITDA margin | −77% | −41% | −17% | −1% | 9% |
| Net income | −$1.17M | −$1.83M | −$1.68M | −$0.71M | $1.15M |
| Free cash flow | −$1.21M | −$1.97M | −$1.76M | −$0.64M | $1.47M |
| Ending cash | $4.29M | $17.32M | $15.56M | $14.92M | $16.39M |

EBITDA turns positive in **Year 5** (v1 showed Year 3, but only by counting the excluded revenue share). Cumulative operating burn through Year 4 is about $5.6M, so the $20M raised leaves a buffer; the round sizes in the funding plan can be revisited.

## Scenarios

Conservative and Optimistic scale revenue and customers to 70% and 140% of base. The spend plan is the same in every scenario, so the conservative case shows what under-delivering on revenue actually costs.

| Year 5 | Conservative | Base | Optimistic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $14.73M | $21.05M | $29.47M |
| EBITDA | −$3.06M | $1.99M | $8.73M |
| Net income | −$3.90M | $1.15M | $5.91M |
| Ending cash | $5.36M | $16.39M | $28.37M |

In the conservative case the company is still losing about $3M a year in Year 5 and would need to cut spend or raise again.

## Valuation summary

Full method in [Valuation_Analysis.md](Valuation_Analysis.md).

| Method | Conservative | Base | Optimistic |
|---|---|---|---|
| DCF (8× Year-5 EBITDA terminal value) | Not meaningful (negative) | $5.6M | $48.1M |
| 10–15× planned Year-1 ARR | $9.6M–$14.4M | $13.7M–$20.6M | $19.2M–$28.9M |

The model does not support the $45M seed pre-money in the v1 package. Seed pricing for a pre-revenue company is set by the market; use these figures as a sanity check, not a target.

## Revenue deliberately excluded

| Stream | Why it is out | What would bring it back |
|---|---|---|
| Insurance performance partnership / revenue share | Sharing insurance commissions or taking per-placement fees generally requires an insurance license; advisor matching can be a paid referral arrangement | Written opinion from securities and insurance counsel, a named licensed channel partner, and a compliant fee structure (for example a flat technology fee that does not vary with placement) |
| Implementation services | Not in the v1 model either | Pricing and delivery plan |

*This is not legal advice.*

## Risk factors

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Churn above plan | High — LTV falls in direct proportion | Instrument retention from launch; price annual plans |
| Slower acquisition | High — conservative case does not reach break-even | Stage hiring against revenue milestones |
| Regulatory treatment of insurance features | High | Keep insurance outputs illustrative; licensed partner before any compensation tied to placement |
| AI output treated as advice | High | Disclosures, guardrails, supervision log, evaluation suite (in the backend) |
| Competition | Medium | Advisor workflow depth; white-label |
