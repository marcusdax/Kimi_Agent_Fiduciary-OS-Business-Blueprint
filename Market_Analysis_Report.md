## Executive Summary

The financial advisory technology market represents one of the most compelling investment opportunities in fintech today. With a combined addressable market exceeding $140 billion in 2025 and projected to reach $377+ billion by 2030, the sector benefits from demographic tailwinds, regulatory pressures, and technological disruption that favor modern, AI-powered platforms.

Fiduciary OS™ operates at the intersection of four high-growth segments:
1. **Financial Planning Software** ($5.82B, 16.6% CAGR)
2. **RIA Software** ($9.76B, 15.62% CAGR)
3. **Insurtech Solutions** ($10.3B, 31.5% CAGR)
4. **Insurance Platforms** ($116.16B, 12.3% CAGR)

This report provides comprehensive market sizing, growth drivers, customer segmentation, and strategic positioning analysis to inform Fiduciary OS™'s market entry and expansion strategy.

#### TAM Methodology

Fiduciary OS™'s TAM is calculated based on:

1. **B2B Software TAM**: Annual spending by 20,483 U.S. advisors on planning and practice management software
   - Average current spend: $3,000-$5,000/advisor/year
   - Addressable spend with value-added AI: $6,000-$10,000/advisor/year
   - **B2B TAM: $120-205M annually**

2. **B2C Software TAM**: Premium subscriptions from 100M+ underserved households
   - Target penetration: 5-10%
   - Average subscription: $15-30/month
   - **B2C TAM: $9-36B annually**

3. **Partnership/Transaction TAM**: Revenue share on facilitated transactions
   - Annuity market: $250B annually
   - Life insurance market: $150B+ annually
   - Investment flows: $2T+ annually
   - **Partnership TAM: $8-15B annually**
