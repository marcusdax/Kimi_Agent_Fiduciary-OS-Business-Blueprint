## Reference method findings
`calcAnnuity` invents product rates with type constants and arbitrary deferral/joint multipliers; `calcLife` invents carrier premiums. Investment risk ratios derive from preset risk labels, not observations. `runScenario` returns fixed arrays and recovery times. `calcTax` mixes 2024 thresholds with other-year deductions and subtracts unconstrained strategy inputs. Estate uses a stale 13.9M exemption and unconditional gifting × 40%. `callAI` directly calls a third-party endpoint from a browser with no configured authorization. Vault capacity/key statuses and client/meeting lists are illustrative. These are **not** validated live services. Our replacement methods are original, limited and versioned, not a copied endorsement.

## API / method contract
- `GET /api/v1/tools/catalog`: authenticated allowlisted tool definitions, ranges and limitations.
- `GET /api/v1/tools/scenarios`: authenticated user's saved records only; private/no-store.
- `PUT /api/v1/tools/scenarios/:kind`: strict `{version,inputs}`; version 0 first save; additive `ToolScenario` table; 409 on conflict. No userId accepted. Bounded source/date and numeric precision/range; non-finite/unsupported output rejected before writing.
- `GET /api/v1/tools/observations`: authenticated, rate-limited fixed provider adapters; no user-supplied URL, asset identifier or upstream credential; only public fixed series sent. 8s timeout, no redirects, 1MB response cap, schema/date checks, coalescing, cache, 60s failure backoff. Client marks cached data stale as TTL expires. No observations substituted for personal facts or return assumptions.
- Annual investment recurrence: `(balance × (1+return) × (1-fee)) + contribution` at year end, floored at zero. Retirement applies constant nominal annual SSA/pension and inflation-escalated spending at year end; cumulative shortfall separate. Not equivalent to prior monthly saved-plan method: both versions are labeled and independent.
- Annuity: effective annual rate converted to monthly; term-certain present-value formula, zero-rate limit tested. Life needs are undiscounted entered replacement-income years + debts + education − entered available assets/coverage, floored at zero. Risk is weighted one-period entered shocks; cash unshocked. No legal/tax/advice certification.
